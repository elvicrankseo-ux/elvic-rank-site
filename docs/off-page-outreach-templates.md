# Off-Page Outreach Templates

**These are drafts for the owner to review, personalize, and send.** No
message using these templates has been sent by this process. Every
template avoids spam patterns, guaranteed-ranking claims, and generic
filler — each requires the sender to fill in a real, specific detail
before sending (marked `[BRACKETS]`). Do not send any of these as-is
without filling in the specifics and confirming the target is real and
relevant.

---

## 1. Resource-Page Outreach

**Use when:** a page curates links to useful resources on a topic our
guide genuinely fits (e.g. "local SEO resources," "small business
marketing guides").

> Subject: Resource suggestion for [PAGE TITLE]
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I came across your resource page on [PAGE TOPIC] at [PAGE URL] while
> researching [SPECIFIC TOPIC] — it's a genuinely useful list.
>
> We recently put together a practical guide on [local citations / a
> technical SEO checklist — pick the actually relevant one]:
> [ARTICLE URL]. It covers [ONE SPECIFIC THING THE GUIDE COVERS THAT
> WOULD FIT THIS PAGE], which seemed like it could be a fit alongside
> what you've already got listed.
>
> No pressure either way — just thought it might be useful to your
> readers. Thanks for putting the list together.
>
> [SENDER NAME]
> Elvic Rank — https://elvicrank.com

> [SENDER NAME]

## 3. Expert-Commentary Pitch

**Use when:** a journalist or publication is soliciting expert quotes on
a local SEO / digital marketing story (e.g. via a source-request platform).

> Subject: Source for [STORY TOPIC]
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> Saw your request for sources on [STORY TOPIC]. I work in local SEO for
> service businesses at Elvic Rank — happy to offer a quote if useful.

> Elvic Rank — https://elvicrank.com

## 4. Partnership / Collaboration Pitch

business or vendor — e.g. a web developer, CRM tool, or scheduling
software relevant to local service businesses.

> start with something informal, like linking to each other's resource

## 5. Case-Study / Reference Pitch

**Use when:** asking an existing client if they'd be willing to be
featured or provide a reference — **only once real, measurable results
exist,** not before. Do not send this for a campaign that's still in
progress.

> Subject: Would you be open to being featured as a case study?
>
> Hi [CLIENT CONTACT NAME],
>
> Now that we've got real results from the work on [SERVICE], I wanted
> to ask — would you be open to us writing it up as a short case study
> on our site? We'd only use numbers and details you're comfortable
> sharing publicly, and we'd send you the draft to approve before
> anything goes live.
>
> No obligation at all if you'd rather not — just thought the results
> were worth sharing if you're open to it.
>
> [SENDER NAME]
> Elvic Rank — https://elvicrank.com

## General Rules for Using These Templates
1. Never send to a target that hasn't been individually researched (see `docs/phase-20-off-page-execution.md` §Outreach Targets).
2. Never claim a ranking, traffic, or result that hasn't actually been measured.
3. Never use "I found your website and loved it" or similar generic filler — every template above requires a real, specific detail.
4. Never promise guaranteed rankings, guaranteed leads, or guaranteed results to the target or their audience.
5. Personalize every bracketed section — a template sent unedited reads as spam and damages the domain's reputation.
