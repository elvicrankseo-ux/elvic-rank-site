# Towing Outreach Templates

Drafts for the owner to personalize and send — **no message using
these templates has been sent by this process.** Every template
requires filling in a real, specific detail actually observed during
the audit (`docs/towing-seo-audit-framework.md`) before sending.
Never send one of these unedited, and never state a finding
("your Google Business Profile is missing X") unless it was actually
observed for that specific business.

**Standing rules for all templates below** (same discipline already
used in `docs/off-page-outreach-templates.md`):
1. Never claim a ranking, traffic, or result that hasn't been measured.
2. Never say something negative or alarmist ("your rankings are
   terrible") unless the audit specifically proves it, and even then,
   prefer a neutral, specific observation over an alarming one.
3. Every claim must be something actually observed for that business,
   not a generic industry statistic.
4. No fake urgency, no fake discounts, no guaranteed outcomes.

## General Rules

1. Never send to a business that hasn't actually been looked at first
   — every template's bracketed detail must be real.
2. Never use generic filler ("I love what you're doing!") — every
   template requires a specific, verified detail.
3. Never promise a guaranteed ranking, call volume, or result.
4. Space follow-ups out (§9 of `docs/elvic-rank-prospecting-machine.md`)
   — day 3, day 7, day 14, then the breakup message.
5. Log every response (or non-response) in the prospect database
   (`docs/elvic-rank-prospecting-machine.md` §12) so the pipeline stage
   stays accurate.
