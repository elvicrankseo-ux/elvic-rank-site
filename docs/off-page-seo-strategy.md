# Off-Page SEO & Authority-Building Strategy

**This is a strategy and documentation deliverable only.** No accounts were
created, no listings were submitted, no profiles were claimed, no outreach
was sent, and no backlinks were acquired or purchased as part of this
document. Every action below marked as an opportunity requires the site
owner's own participation, credentials, and judgment.

## 1. Current Authority Status

**[D — codebase/site finding]** Elvic Rank is a newly-launched agency site
with no existing off-page footprint that this codebase or a live-site
review can detect. There is no evidence (in the codebase or via live
inspection) of existing backlinks, directory listings, press mentions, or
third-party profiles. This is expected and normal for a new site — it is
not treated as a defect.

## 2. Evidence Available
- Full site content (14 service pages, 8 blog articles) — real, reviewed directly.
- `siteConfig` (name, email, WhatsApp, social handles, service-area description) — real, reviewed directly.
- One real, named case study in progress (Akanaby Logistics Inc. — Towing & Logistics; Website Design completed, GBP Optimization in progress, SEO Campaign not yet started) — reviewed directly in `src/components/sections/case-studies.tsx`.
- Homepage "About" section — reviewed directly (`src/components/sections/about.tsx`): communicates philosophy/positioning (4 stated principles) but does not name a founder or individual.

## 3. Evidence Unavailable
- Any backlink data (no Ahrefs/Moz/Semrush-equivalent access in this environment — cannot report referring domains, domain rating, or existing backlink count; **not measurable in this environment**, not zero by assumption).
- Any existing citation/directory presence — not checked, since checking would require searching for and potentially interacting with third-party sites on the business's behalf, which is outside this task's scope (documentation only).

## 5. Missing Linkable Assets

**[D]** No original data/research, no interactive tool or calculator, no
downloadable template exists. This is a genuine gap in the classic
"digital PR asset" sense (journalists and industry sites disproportionately
link to original data, not advice content). **This is explicitly NOT being
created this phase** — there is no GSC or usage evidence yet to justify the
effort a genuinely good data study or tool would require, and building one
speculatively risks exactly the "activity without evidence" pattern this
project has consistently avoided. Flagged as a **future** opportunity only,
to revisit once real traffic/query data exists to guide its topic.

## 6. Citation Readiness

| Field | Status |
|---|---|
| Business name | Consistent everywhere: "Elvic Rank" |
| Website | Consistent: `https://elvicrank.com` |
| Email | Consistent: `info@elvicrank.com` |
| Phone | **Not set** — `siteConfig.phone` is intentionally `null`. Many directories require a phone number; this is a real, pre-existing limitation (flagged in Phase 11/12) unaffected by this phase. |
| Address | **None** — deliberate, since Elvic Rank operates as a remote/service-area business, not a storefront. Compatible with directories that support "service area business" listings (e.g. Google Business Profile's SAB format); incompatible with directories that require a physical street address. |
| Service area | Stated clearly: US, Canada, UK, Australia, and Africa (`siteConfig.location.servingLine`) |
| Social profiles | Instagram, X, TikTok (no LinkedIn or Facebook currently) |

**Conclusion:** business identity is internally consistent and ready for
citation building on service-area-compatible directories. The missing
phone number and address rule out directories that mandate them — this is
a real constraint, not something this phase can resolve (it requires the
business's actual phone number, which doesn't exist yet per prior phases).

## 7. Directory Opportunity Categories

| Category | Relevance | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| General business directories (e.g. a Google Business Profile listing, Bing Places) | HIGH — foundational, service-area compatible | Owner verification, no address needed for SAB format |
| Digital marketing / SEO agency directories (agency-listing sites, marketing-service directories) | HIGH — directly relevant industry, likely to also drive referral traffic | Owner sign-up, business description, may request portfolio/case studies |
| Startup / small-business directories | MEDIUM — relevant to a new agency, lower direct SEO value | Owner sign-up |
| Local chamber of commerce / business associations | LOW for a remote/multi-country agency — these are typically tied to a specific physical locality Elvic Rank doesn't have | Physical presence or clearly defined local market, which doesn't currently apply |
| Professional/industry organizations (e.g. marketing associations) | MEDIUM — relevant to credibility, often membership-based | Owner sign-up, possibly a fee |

named founder/expert identity (a real decision only the site owner can
make — this is not something to fabricate).

## 11. Resource-Page Opportunities

**[D]** "Resource pages" (pages that curate useful links for a specific
audience — e.g. "SEO resources for local businesses") are a legitimate,
white-hat link target for genuinely useful content like the Local Citations
and Technical SEO Checklist guides (§4). Pursuing this requires manually
searching for and reaching out to page owners — **not performed this
phase**, listed as a MEDIUM-priority manual action for the owner.

## 13. What NOT To Do

Explicitly ruled out, consistent with this project's standing rules across
every phase: paid spam backlinks, automated backlink software, PBNs, link
farms, mass directory spam, comment/forum spam, fake reviews, fake
citations, fake businesses or addresses, fabricated author profiles,
fabricated awards, fabricated clients or case studies, fabricated
statistics, AI-generated fake testimonials, and irrelevant bulk guest-post
schemes. None of these were used or considered viable.
